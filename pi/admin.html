Bands

A locked band can still be listened to. The edges are the US ones: change them to your own limits or license. The lock works for the panel, WSJT-X and other programs. It can't stop the microphone or key plugged into the radio itself.

High-SWR shutdown

While the radio transmits, its meters are read about 5 times a second. On high SWR the radio is un-keyed and the band stays locked until someone resets it. It is a backup: tune the antenna first and set the radio's own power sensibly.
