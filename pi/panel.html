✉ Open the mailbox (Pat)

      
Winlink runs on the Pi: xara (a VARA HF modem) and Pat (the mail program). Open the mailbox, write or read mail, then
        Connect → VARA HF and pick a gateway: Pat tunes the radio. Pat asks for your Winlink password the first time; it stays on the Pi.
        While Winlink runs the voice client can't connect (one audio link at a time) and the radio stays in DIG. Every transmit lock and safety check still applies.
